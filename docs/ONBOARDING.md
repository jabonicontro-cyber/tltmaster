# Company onboarding and data import

How a trucking company gets its workspace ready in Taviron, what exists today, and what comes next (master plan §12
B11). Imports and setup prepare the data that dispatch checks, Smart Dispatch and **Plan the day** rely on, so
missing data is always shown as missing.

## 1. What exists

| Step | Who | Where | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Create the company | Taviron team | `/admin/onboarding` (platform owners only) | No users yet; never touches another company (IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN §5m). |
| Import customers, materials, trucks, drivers from CSV | Taviron team | `/admin/onboarding/[company]` | Header matching with manual mapping; values normalised; every row checked in the browser, on the server and in the database. Staging creates nothing; rows are *ready*, *possible duplicate* or *needs fixing*. **Approve** creates the chosen rows all at once or not at all. **Revert** removes only rows the batch created that are untouched and unused (others are kept and deactivated). Every step is audited in `private.platform_audit`. |
| Owner login | Taviron team | `scripts/create-owner.mjs` / invitation (DEPLOYMENT §3) | |
| Invite people and set roles | Company owner | People (Settings → Users now opens People) | Server and database enforce roles. |
| **Company setup checklist** (new, 2026-10-13) | Company owner (`settings.manage`) | `/setup` | Read-only. Company profile, people and roles, customers, materials and standard rates, trucks and capacity, trailers (fleets with tractors), drivers and qualifications — each *done*, *incomplete* or *not started*, with counts of what is missing and a link to fix it. Built from the company's own data (RLS); if anything cannot be read, the page says so instead of showing an all-clear. Logic: `src/lib/onboarding/readiness.ts` (tested in `tests/unit/onboarding-readiness.test.ts`); data: `src/server/data/readiness.ts`. |

Demo companies: the checklist works the same way in every demo company for the Owner mode (other modes have no
`settings.manage`); it reflects the demo's fictional data and changes nothing.

## 2. Rules

- Never invent missing customer, driver, vehicle, load or financial information; never treat inferred values as
  confirmed. A suggestion (human or, later, AI) is labelled as a suggestion until a person confirms it.
- Imports never silently overwrite existing records; duplicates are shown and decided by a person.
- Reversal is promised only where it is safe (untouched, unused records); otherwise the record is deactivated and the
  limitation is stated before approval.
- Tenant isolation: platform imports run through `platform_*` functions that check the platform role and the target
  company; company users only ever see their own company.

## 3. Next steps (in order)

1. **Self-service CSV import for company administrators** using the same staging → review → approve → revert model,
   scoped to the administrator's own company. Needs company-scoped database functions (a migration the owner applies);
   until then imports stay with the Taviron team.
2. Contacts, job sites, customer rates and driver qualifications as import types; XLSX files.
3. AI-assisted mapping and error explanations through the shared AI platform (backlog B6) once a provider is approved —
   suggestions only, confirmed by a person.
4. A setup link from the Command Center while the checklist is incomplete.

## 4. Guided onboarding checklist (B68, 2026-10-19 — migration `20261120001000`, local only)

**Status:** implemented and locally tested; **blocked on migration** (`20261120001000_guided_onboarding`, not applied
hosted). Before it is applied both views say "needs the database update" instead of showing an empty checklist.

**What it is.** One checklist per company, shared by the company and Taviron, generated by the database
(`private.onboarding_generate`) from what it knows: plan (lifecycle plan, or the demo plan), trucks, tractors and
trailers, drivers, dispatch bases, the customer-portal entitlement, and the integrations the server reports as
configured (HERE routing, AI — booleans only). Generating again only adds tasks that newly apply and refreshes the
instructions of tasks nobody has started; it never removes a task or changes a status.

**Tasks** have instructions, who acts (*Your team* / *Taviron* / *Together*), whether Taviron reviews, required or
optional, dependencies, an owner and due date, files, and a full history. States: Not started, In progress, Awaiting
customer, Submitted for review, Changes requested, Approved (Completed when no review), Blocked, Not applicable.

| Who | Where | May |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company (settings.manage) | `/setup` → *Onboarding with Taviron* | Start, submit a reviewed task, withdraw a submission, complete a task that needs no review, mark blocked (reason required), choose an owner and due date, upload files, message Taviron. Never approve a reviewed task, set not applicable, reopen a finished task, or act on Taviron's tasks. |
| Taviron (platform admin, aal2) | `/admin/onboarding/[company]` | Start / update the checklist, approve, request changes (reason), awaiting customer, blocked (reason), not applicable (reason for required tasks), reopen, set due dates, internal notes (never shown to the company), in-app reminders (once a day per task), open a company's CSV in the importer. |

Rules enforced in the database (`private.onboarding_change`): approving or submitting needs every dependency approved or
not applicable; reasons are required where stated; the company side is limited to its current company.

**Documents.** PDF, JPEG, PNG or UTF-8 CSV up to 10 MB, 20 per task; content type checked from the bytes in the app and
again against the stored object in the database; private bucket `onboarding-documents`, path chosen by the database
inside the company's folder; downloads are 60-second signed links after a database check, served as attachments.

**Imports.** A CSV on a task with an import type (customers, materials, trucks, drivers) has *Import this file…* for
Taviron: it opens the existing importer (§1) with the file loaded — mapping, validation errors, duplicate detection and
the explicit approval step are unchanged, and the task history records that a batch was staged. Nothing is created until
the batch is approved. Self-service import for company administrators is still §3 step 1.

**Notifications.** In-app only (`comm_notifications`, category `onboarding`): to the task's owner, or the company's
owners when nobody is assigned, on changes requested, awaiting customer, approval, Taviron replies and reminders.
Nothing is emailed.

**Lifecycle and billing.** The checklist never changes the lifecycle (stage, go-live date, fees) or billing. Since the
Onboarding Kit (§5) activation of a company *with a checklist* also waits for the signed go-live acceptance. When every required task is approved or not applicable the checklist says
so; Taviron then agrees the go-live date on the lifecycle page as before. Tested (`tests/db/guided-onboarding.test.ts`,
`tests/e2e/guided-onboarding.spec.ts`).

**AI.** None in this slice. A later summary would only explain progress and never approve or change a task.

**Demo companies.** Same code. Refresh demo data adds a fictional mid-onboarding checklist once
(`public.onboarding_demo_samples`, `is_demo` only, idempotent) with finished, submitted, changes-requested, blocked
(overdue), awaiting-customer and not-started tasks; the Owner mode sees it on `/setup` (other modes have no
settings.manage). Nothing is sent. Checked in `tests/e2e/demo-parity.spec.ts` and `tests/db/guided-onboarding.test.ts`.

**Not yet:** manual (non-generated) tasks, task templates per plan beyond the generated set, an onboarding view on the
platform Launchpad's attention queue, emailed reminders (needs an email decision), AI summaries.

## 5. Onboarding Kit (B75, 2026-10-20 — migration `20261120001500`, local only)

**Status:** implemented and locally tested (DB 5, unit 5, browser 3); **blocked on migration** `20261120001500`
(Phase B of the hosted runbook). Before it is applied the kit pages say the database update is needed.

**Where.** Company: `/setup/kit` (settings.manage; linked from `/setup` → *Open Onboarding*). Taviron:
`/admin/onboarding/[company]/kit` (platform admin, aal2). One shared record per company; sections Overview, Welcome
email, Questionnaire, Data import, Dispatch worksheet, Integrations, Testing, Go-live acceptance, After launch.

| Deliverable | What it is | Who edits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome email | Template filled from the company record; **copy only, never sent** (no email is sent by the kit). Says billing starts at the actual go-live. | Taviron |
| Questionnaire | Company profile, operations, fleet, people, customers, billing contacts; missing answers are listed. | Company and Taviron |
| Import templates | Header-only CSVs generated from the importer's own column list (`templateCsv`), downloaded at `/onboarding/templates/[entity]`; uploading goes through the existing reviewed import. | — |
| Dispatch worksheet | One card per dispatch rule with the product's real enforcement (verified against `src/lib/dispatch/smart.ts`: hours of service is *manually checked*, bases, eligibility, availability, maintenance and safety are *product-enforced*), the company's rule, and test scenarios with results. | Company and Taviron |
| Integration checklist | Each integration's real availability (accounting, ELD and payments are *not available*); the company records needs, Taviron records checks; "verified" is refused for anything not configured on the server. **No secrets:** keys such as password, token, secret or api_key are refused by the server and the database. | Company (needs), Taviron (checks) |
| Go-live acceptance | Twelve items; four are critical (tenant permissions, data validation, dispatch testing, safety procedures) and can be neither waived nor excepted. Exceptions need a reason and are listed in the sign-off. Open critical testing issues block sign-off. | Taviron records results; the company owner and Taviron sign |

**Rules in the database.** Every save carries the version it was based on; a stale save is refused (`40001`) and every
version is kept (`onboarding_form_versions`). Sign-offs are append-only, tied to the acceptance version, and voided
(kept in history) by any later change. Only an owner signs for the company, never in a demo company.
`private.lifecycle_activate_due()` skips, and *Go Live Now* refuses, a company that has an onboarding checklist and no
accepted go-live; nothing else about lifecycle, fees or billing changes.

**Demo companies.** Same pages and data; the owner's sign-off panel is not offered and the database refuses it
(docs/DEMO_TENANTS.md §2). No kit sample data yet: a demo shows the empty kit with its real rules.

**Rollback** `supabase/rollbacks/20261120001500_onboarding_kit.down.sql` restores the earlier activation functions and
drops every kit form, version and sign-off (destructive).

**Not yet:** kit sample data for demos, emailing the welcome email (needs an email decision), PDF export of the signed
acceptance.
